Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes: free, no account to create and no credit card.

Does it ask for my passwords?

No, never. You sign in to your job sites yourself, in your own browser.

Does it apply to everything?

No. It only applies to the offers that fit the rules you wrote, and it tells you why it dropped the others.

Which browsers?

Chrome, Edge and Brave (Chromium-based browsers).
